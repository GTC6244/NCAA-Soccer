The program

  • Founded 1975. Fourth head coach in program history; Hudson is 115-44-23 across 11 seasons.
  • Won the first ACC tournament title in program history in 2025 and opened 2026 at 5-0-1, ranked No. 7.
  • 37 NCAA appearances, College Cups in 2000 and 2005, Elite Eights in 2010, 2019 and 2024.
  • The 2023 side produced four All-Americans — most of any Division I program that year.
  • Washburne Stadium, capacity 2,577. Dedicated soccer venue, ten home matches.

Academics

  • 11:1 ratio, average class size 22, 70% of classes at 25 or fewer. Best-in-class teaching for a school this size.
  • Cox School of Business ranked #25 nationally — but direct-admit, so you must name business as your primary interest on the application.
  • Computer science is not a restricted major. A real edge over Penn State, Ohio State, UCSB and NJIT, which all gate it behind a second application.

Place

  • Not a college town. 234 walkable acres in University Park, an affluent enclave inside Dallas.
  • Two airports: Love Field 15 minutes, DFW 30–35. Strong routing for a Canadian family.
  • Residential Commons system, two years on campus required.

Known for George W. Bush Presidential Center is on campus. Peruna, a live Shetland pony, is the mascot. Only school ever given the NCAA death penalty (football, 1987).

The program

  • Calabrese is in his 10th season. Won the first Sun Belt tournament title in program history in 2025.
  • That run was entirely away: 3-2 in 2OT at Georgia Southern, 1-0 at top-seeded Kentucky, then Marshall — the Herd's first home loss in 721 days.

  • Round of 16 in 2019 and 2020. Twelve NCAA appearances.

Academics

  • Largest university in Florida — 70,674 students, 59,649 undergraduates.
  • The trade-off is scale: 28:1 ratio, only 28% of classes under 20 students. The inverse of SMU.

Place

  • 1,420 acres, 13 miles east of downtown Orlando. Large and spread out, ringed by student apartments.
  • Orlando International is 20–25 minutes with direct Toronto service — one of the best air links here.
  • ~12,140 beds on campus, mostly first-years. Huge adjacent rental market.

Known for Deep ties to the space and simulation industries. Rosen College of Hospitality is nationally leading. Knightro the knight.

The program

  • ~900 miles to the border — the first school where driving is conceivable.

The program

  • Rob Dow was born in Cole Harbour, Nova Scotia. He will read a Canadian player's background with more context than most.
  • At Vermont he went 129-41-21, made five consecutive NCAA tournaments and won the 2024 national championship — the only America East coach ever to do it.
  • Penn State puts him at the ninth-best win percentage among active Division I men's coaches.
  • He inherited a hard job. Penn State finished last in the Big Ten in each of the past two seasons — 5-8-4 overall, 1-6-3 in conference last year.
  • The program was strong recently: Big Ten tournament champions in 2019 and 2020, ranked as high as No. 11.
  • Jeffrey Field. Crowds are modest — a few hundred for conference matches.

Academics

  • #59 nationally, #26 among publics. 7,958 acres, the largest campus here.
  • 89% of faculty are full-time against a 47% national average. But intro courses run in 300-seat lecture halls.
  • Smeal Business, Engineering and Schreyer Honors are direct-admit and far more selective than the university's headline rate. Computer science sits inside Engineering, so it carries that bar.

Place

  • The most genuine college town on either list, and the most isolated. State College is 42,000 people existing because of the university.
  • ~250 miles to Buffalo–Niagara, about 4.5 hours. Second-best border access on the Core 12. Toronto is about 6 hours.
  • University Park Airport is 10 minutes but small, with no direct international service. Pittsburgh is 2.5 hours.

Known for One of the largest alumni networks in the world. THON, the largest student philanthropy anywhere. Beaver Stadium holds 106,000. The Nittany Lion.

The program

  • 2006 NCAA national champions, 2004 runners-up. The only national champion on either list.
  • Over 60 players have gone professional or represented senior national teams — the best conversion rate in this research.
  • NCAA attendance champions nine straight years, 2007–2015 — the longest streak in the record books. They hold the top six all-time on-campus attendance records.
  • Peak crowd: 15,896 against UCLA in 2010, a night the fire marshal turned fans away.
  • Vom Steeg is in his 25th season. Harder Stadium holds 17,000 — the largest venue on either list.

  • #40 nationally, #14 among publics. 56.5% of classes under 20. 85% four-year graduation — the highest here.

  • Quarter system, not semesters. And the UC system is test-blind — scores are not considered at all.

Place

  • Literally on the beach — a bluff above the Pacific with ocean on two sides and a lagoon through the middle.
  • Isla Vista, the adjacent student town, is one of the densest residential communities in the US — convenient, and with a party reputation worth discussing openly.
  • Santa Barbara airport is small; LAX is two hours. ~1,200 miles to the border, 18–19 hours.

Known for Kavli Institute for Theoretical Physics. One of the most beautiful campuses in the country. The College of Creative Studies. Michael Douglas is an alumnus.

  • Six national championships (1977, 2008, 2010, 2014, 2015, 2025) and seven OUA titles. Five of the nationals are Isacco's.
  • Isacco played Division I soccer at Maryland — three-time All-ACC first team goalkeeper, team captain in 1992, Canadian Olympic Team. The only coach here who came through the American system and now coaches in Canada.
  • The roster is a professional pathway: two players from Toronto FC II (MLS Next Pro), plus Vaughan SC in League1 Ontario, TMU Bold, Utah Tech (NCAA D1) and a US junior college.
  • York Lions Stadium is also home to York United FC in the Canadian Premier League.
  • Eight coaches on staff — the deepest here. Four publish emails.

Academics

  • Canada's third-largest university, ~55,000 students in north Toronto plus the Glendon bilingual campus.
  • Schulich School of Business is elite, and its BBA is direct-entry — a competitive admission decision rather than a post-first-year hurdle. Arguably better for an athlete: you know where you stand before you arrive.
  • Osgoode Hall Law School is among Canada's strongest. Lassonde School of Engineering covers computer science.
  • Generally less selective and less grade-deflating than U of T — worth weighing against prestige for an athlete balancing a season.

Place

  • Suburban north Toronto at Keele and Steeles. The TTC Line 1 subway extension runs directly onto campus.
  • More self-contained and isolated in feel than U of T's downtown campus.
  • Pearson is 25 minutes. No flights for home matches; all OUA away fixtures drivable.

Known for Schulich and Osgoode Hall. Canada's most successful modern men's university soccer program. Yeo the Lion.
