# Men's Soccer Recruiting Research

Research on 28 men's college soccer programs across the United States and Canada — NCAA Division I and II, NAIA, and U SPORTS — covering the soccer program, academics, geography and coaching contacts for each.

Compiled September 2026.

## The three tiers

**Core 12** — the primary targets: SMU, UCF, USF, South Carolina, Ohio State, Penn State, Boston University, Northeastern, UC Santa Barbara, NJIT, UMass Lowell, IU Indianapolis.

**Extras** — 13 schools across Florida, Georgia and the Carolinas, added to widen the net.

**Canada** — Toronto, York and Western, all U SPORTS. Different governing body, different scholarship rules, no NCAA eligibility requirement.

## Eight schools have no program

Cincinnati and Appalachian State both cut men's soccer in 2020. Florida, Georgia, UT Austin, Purdue, Miami (FL) and Arizona State have never sponsored it at varsity level. Men's soccer is heavily under-sponsored in the NCAA — roughly 215 Division I men's programs against more than 340 women's.

## Caveats

- Enrolment figures are approximate and not drawn from official fact books.
- Coaching staffs change constantly. Verify before sending anything.
- Kennesaw State is a partial profile. The program exists — ESPN carries a 2026 schedule — but no coach, record or staff contact could be retrieved from public sources. Phone the athletics department.
- Eight schools still need a coach-contact sweep: Florida Atlantic, Clemson, Keiser, Nova Southeastern, UNC Wilmington, UNC Greensboro, UNC Charlotte and Western.

## Two GPA bars

NCAA initial eligibility requires a 2.3 core-course GPA across 16 core courses on a sliding scale with test scores. University admission is a separate and usually much higher bar. U SPORTS has neither requirement; NAIA eligibility rules are more flexible than the NCAA's.
